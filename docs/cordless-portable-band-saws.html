How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $140; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from deep cut (30%), a brushless motor (25%), variable speed (25%), work light (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 2,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

What's a portable band saw for?

Clean, cool, quiet cuts in metal: conduit, pipe, strut, threaded rod and angle. Cuts are square and burr-free with almost no sparks, unlike a grinder.

Deep cut or compact?

Deep-cut saws (4-3/4 to 5 inches) handle large pipe and stock but are heavy. Compact saws (2.5 to 3-3/8 inches) are light enough for one hand and overhead work.

Does blade speed matter?

Slower speeds suit hard metals like stainless; faster speeds cut mild steel and conduit quickly. Variable speed lets you match the material.

Which blade?

Pick teeth per inch for the wall thickness: about 14 TPI for thicker stock, 18 to 24 TPI for thin-wall conduit and tubing. Keep three teeth in the material.

Can it cut wood?

Yes, but these saws are designed for metal. For wood curves, use a jigsaw or a stationary band saw.
