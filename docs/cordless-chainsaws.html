How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $100; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from bar 14 plus (25%), battery included (25%), a brushless motor (20%), tool free tension (15%), auto oil (15%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 7,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

What bar length do I need?

10 to 12 inches handles pruning and limbs up to about 8 inches thick. 14 to 16 inches is the sweet spot for storm cleanup and firewood. 18 inches and up is for bigger trees.

Can a battery saw replace gas?

For most homeowners, yes. EGO rates its 16-inch saws as equal to a 40cc gas saw. Big logs all day still favor gas or a second battery.

How many cuts per charge?

EGO lists up to 130 cuts through a 4x4 on its 2.5Ah battery, Greenworks about 50 on 2.0Ah. Bigger batteries cut longer; buy a spare for firewood days.

What maintenance does it need?

Keep bar oil topped up (most oil automatically), keep the chain sharp and correctly tensioned. Tool-free tensioning makes adjustments easy.

What safety gear do I need?

Eye and ear protection, cut-resistant chaps, gloves and boots. Never cut above shoulder height, and watch the tip of the bar to avoid kickback.
