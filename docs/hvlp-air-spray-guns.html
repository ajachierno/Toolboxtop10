How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $36; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from multi nozzle (30%), regulator (25%), air specs (25%), two guns (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 2,500 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

HVLP or LVLP?

HVLP guns use lots of air at low pressure for good transfer efficiency, so they need a bigger compressor. LVLP guns use less air and run on smaller compressors with similar finish quality.

Which nozzle size?

1.3 to 1.5 mm for basecoat and clearcoat, 1.7 to 2.0 mm for primer and single-stage paint, 2.5 mm and up for high-build primer. Sets with several nozzles cover a whole job.

Do I need a regulator?

Yes. A regulator at the gun lets you set the exact pressure the gun is designed for. A water separator keeps moisture out of the paint.

Compressor size

Check the gun's CFM rating and buy a compressor that delivers more than that continuously. Undersized compressors cause sputtering and poor atomization.

Clean it right away

Run the right solvent through the gun after every use and take apart the nozzle and needle. Dried paint ruins spray patterns.
