How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $60; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from hydraulic (35%), high lift (25%), tie downs (20%), casters (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 5,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Scissor jack or hydraulic jack?

A scissor jack ($55 to $105 here) cranks up with a handle or socket, and it's cheap, light, and reliable. A hydraulic jack ($125 and up) lifts with a foot pedal, usually rolls on casters, and is much faster and easier with a heavy cruiser or bagger. Both get the wheels off the ground; neither puts the bike at standing height.

Will a jack fit under my bike?

Check ground clearance under the frame. Scissor jacks here start at 3.5 to 3.7 in. Most hydraulic jacks need 4.5 to 4.9 in., and the OTC 1545 starts at 3.5 in. Bikes with full lower fairings or exhausts under the frame may need a jack with adapters or a lift table.

How high does it need to go?

To pull a rear wheel you usually need the tire 2 to 4 in. off the ground, which most jacks here manage at 13 to 16 in. If you want to work standing up, you need a lift table like the APlusLift, or the VEVOR 350 lb lift for dirt bikes (up to 36 in.).

Do I need tie-down straps?

Yes. A bike on a center jack can tip sideways when you break loose an axle nut. The VEVOR and VIVOHOME hydraulic jacks and the OTC 1545 include ratchet straps; for the others, run straps from the handlebars to the floor or a wall anchor.

Is a 1,100 lb rating enough?

For nearly every motorcycle, yes. Big touring bikes run around 900 lb wet. The 1,500 lb jacks give more margin for heavy baggers and ATVs.
