The 10 Best Rivet Guns

Manual pop rivet guns and hand riveters for sheet metal, gutters, trailers and auto work, ranked from live Amazon data.

A hand rivet gun sets blind (pop) rivets from one side of the work, so it joins sheet metal, gutters, trailer skins and plastic where you can't reach the back. How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $20; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from rivets included (25%), quarter inch (20%), a carrying case (20%), drill bits (20%), long warranty (15%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 11,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

What size rivet gun do I need?

Match the gun to the rivet diameter. Most one-hand guns take 3/32, 1/8, 5/32 and 3/16-inch rivets, which covers gutters, flashing and light auto work. For 1/4-inch rivets on trailers and truck bodies, get a long-handle two-hand riveter like the Astro 1423 or NEXTACK NT300.

Can a hand riveter set steel or stainless rivets?

Aluminum rivets are easy. Steel and stainless take much more force: Arrow says not to use 3/16-inch steel or any stainless rivets in its manual tools, while the long-handle Astro and NEXTACK are rated for 1/4-inch stainless. Check the listing before you buy rivets.

How long should the rivet be?

The rivet's grip range has to cover the total thickness of the parts you're joining. Too short and it won't set; too long and it sets loose. Kits with several lengths, like the NEXTACK's eight sizes, make it easier to match thick stacks.

What drill bit do I use?

Drill a hole just over the rivet diameter, usually the bit listed for that rivet size. Kits from WETOLS, LIBRATON, GIANTISAN and KLRStec include a bit for each rivet size.

Is a rivet gun the same as a rivet nut tool?

No. A rivet gun sets blind rivets, which are permanent. A rivet nut tool sets threaded inserts you can bolt into. The Astro and NEXTACK listings both note they're not for rivet nuts.
