How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $12; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from dual temp (30%), full size (25%), sticks (25%), cordless (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 25,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Mini or full-size glue gun?

Mini guns (0.27-inch sticks) are light and precise for crafts, cards and small repairs. Full-size guns (about 0.44-inch sticks) push much more glue, which you need for wood, larger repairs and long beads.

Why does dual temperature matter?

Low heat protects foam, fabric, ribbon and fingers. High heat bonds stronger to wood, plastic and metal. Dual-temp guns cover both.

How many watts do I need?

20 W mini guns handle crafts. 60-100 W full-size guns keep up with steady gluing. 150-200 W guns are for production work and heavy repairs.

Are cordless glue guns worth it?

For gluing on ladders, outdoors or around a room, yes. Base-heated guns stay hot only a few minutes off the stand; battery guns run longer.

How do I avoid burns and strings?

Use the stand, keep fingers clear of the nozzle, and try low temperature for delicate work. Drip-free copper nozzles cut down on strings.
