Gift-buying questions

What is a good tool gift under $50?

For someone just starting out, a quality screwdriver set, a 25-foot tape measure, or a set of pliers gets used every week. For someone who already has the basics, a corded power tool under $50, like a random orbital sander or heat gun, is a real upgrade.

Are cheap power tools a good gift?

Corded power tools are the best value under $50, because there's no battery to pay for. Under $50, cordless tools are usually bare tools or very basic kits, so we only list cordless picks here that come ready to use.

What if they already have the tool?

Every gift links to Amazon, where you can add a gift receipt at checkout. Tape measures, pliers, and screwdrivers are hard to own too many of, and those make safe picks.
