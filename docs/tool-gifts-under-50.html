Gift-buying questions

What is a good tool gift under $50?

For someone just starting out, a quality screwdriver set, a 25-foot tape measure, or a set of pliers gets used every week. For someone who already has the basics, a corded power tool under $50, like a random orbital sander or heat gun, is a real upgrade.

Are cheap power tools a good gift?

Corded power tools are the best value under $50, because there's no battery to pay for. Under $50, cordless tools are usually bare tools or very basic kits, so we only list cordless picks here that come ready to use.

What's a good tool gift under $50 for someone who has everything?

Measuring and testing tools. A digital caliper, a multimeter, a stud finder, a laser level or a click torque wrench are small, genuinely useful, and often missing from an otherwise full toolbox. Each has a pick under $50 on this page.

What are good car gifts under $50?

An OBD2 scanner that explains the check engine light, a cordless tire inflator for the trunk, or a pair of jack stands for someone who works on their own car. All three categories have a pick on this page.

Do the air tools here need anything else?

Yes. Air nailers, impact wrenches and ratchets run on compressed air, so they're only a good gift for someone who already owns an air compressor. Those cards are marked "Needs an air compressor".

What if they already have the tool?

Every gift links to Amazon, where you can add a gift receipt at checkout. Tape measures, pliers, and screwdrivers are hard to own too many of, and those make safe picks.
