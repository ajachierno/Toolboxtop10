How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $30; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from dual power (30%), accurate (25%), high flow (25%), platform (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 30,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Will a cordless inflator fill a flat tire?

Most will, slowly. Small pumps are built for top-offs and may need a recharge to fill a large tire from flat. High-flow units like the ETENWOLF S6 or a tool-battery inflator handle flats and truck tires better.

What PSI do I need?

Car tires run 30-40 PSI and light trucks up to 80. 150 PSI only matters for road-bike tires. Airflow (CFM or L/min) decides how fast it fills.

How accurate are the gauges?

Most are within ±2 PSI; the best are ±1. Check against a good handheld gauge once, then trust the auto shut-off.

Built-in battery or tool battery?

Built-in battery inflators are small and fit a glovebox. Tool-battery inflators share packs with your drill and usually move more air, but they're bigger.

Can I use it in cold weather?

Yes, but batteries lose capacity in the cold. Keep a dual-power model that also plugs into the car's 12V socket for winter.
