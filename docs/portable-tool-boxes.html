How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 50% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 15% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $40; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from a stackable design (25%), locking drawers (20%), metal latches (20%), water resistance (20%), a removable tray (15%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 15,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Cantilever box, stackable case, or drawer box?

A cantilever-lid box (Stanley, Craftsman) is the cheapest, lightest way to carry a mixed load with small parts up top. A stackable modular case (TSTAK, PACKOUT, ToughSystem) costs more but locks together and travels as a tower. A portable steel drawer box gives you real drawer organization you can still carry. Match the style to how the box moves.

Do I need a weather-sealed box?

If your tools ride in an open truck bed or sit on a wet job site, an IP65-rated box like the PACKOUT or ToughSystem keeps rain and dust out. For a garage or a covered van, a plain plastic box is fine and far cheaper.

How much should it hold?

Load ratings run from about 30 lb for a light plastic hand box to 110 lb for a ToughSystem case. Be honest about what you carry — a box packed with sockets and a drill kit gets heavy, and an over-stuffed light box will crack a latch or bow the lid.

Metal latches or plastic?

Latches are the first thing to fail on a cheap box. Metal or metal-reinforced latches (even the sub-$30 Stanley and Craftsman have them) last far longer than all-plastic clasps, especially in the cold.

Should I buy into a stacking system?

Only if you'll use more than one box. The value of TSTAK, PACKOUT, and ToughSystem is that boxes and organizers click together and roll on a shared cart. Buy a single box from a system and you pay the system premium without the payoff.
