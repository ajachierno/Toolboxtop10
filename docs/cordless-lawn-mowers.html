How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $200; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from wide deck (30%), self propelled (25%), a brushless motor (25%), two batteries (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 12,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

What deck size do I need?

16 to 17 inches suits small lawns and tight spaces. 21 inches is the standard for medium and large lawns and cuts the job down by about a quarter.

How much lawn per charge?

Makers quote coverage on flat, dry grass. Greenworks rates its 16-inch 40V model at 1/3 acre and its 80V self-propelled at 1/2 acre. Thick or wet grass cuts that a lot; a second battery helps.

Do I need self-propelled?

On slopes or lawns over about 1/4 acre, yes. Self-propelled mowers drive the wheels so you only steer.

Brushless worth it?

Brushless motors run cooler and get more mowing from each charge. They're standard on the 60V and 80V Greenworks and EGO mowers here.

Battery platform matters

Mower batteries are expensive. Pick a platform (EGO, Greenworks, WORX) whose trimmers and blowers you'll also use, so one charger and set of packs runs everything.
