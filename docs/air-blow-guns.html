How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $11; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from safety nozzle (30%), specs stated (25%), metal body (25%), extra nozzles (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 12,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

What's an OSHA safety nozzle?

OSHA limits compressed air used for cleaning to under 30 PSI if the nozzle is blocked against skin. Safety nozzles vent air sideways so they can't build that pressure. Required at work, smart at home.

Rubber tip or metal?

Rubber tips won't scratch paint or chrome and seal against holes for blowing out passages. Metal tips are more durable.

Extensions

Long nozzles reach into engine bays, radiators and machine beds without getting your hand in the way.

What fitting?

Almost all blow guns use a 1/4-inch NPT inlet. Add the plug that matches your hose coupler (industrial, automotive or ARO).

Safety

Wear eye protection, never point a blow gun at anyone, and never use it to clean dust off your skin or clothes.
