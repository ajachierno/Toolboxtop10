Klein Tools 32500 vs Amartisan 10-Piece Magnetic

Our Best Overall vs our Best Budget screwdriver sets — is the upgrade worth it?

The short answer

The Klein Tools 32500 costs $5.98 more (60% more) than the Amartisan 10-Piece Magnetic — $15.97 vs $9.99. For the extra money you get: nut drivers. Meanwhile the Amartisan 10-Piece Magnetic has magnetic tips, which the Klein Tools 32500 doesn't.

Buyers rate them 4.8 vs 4.6 stars, and the Klein Tools 32500 has more reviews behind its rating (28,400 vs 12,900). On our 100-point scale that works out to 83 vs 81.

Bottom line: only 2 points separate them, so the Amartisan 10-Piece Magnetic is the smarter buy unless you specifically need nut drivers.
