How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $35; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from cfm 600 (30%), variable speed (25%), ac power (25%), weather rated (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 9,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

How much airflow do I need?

200 CFM is personal cooling at a bench. 600 to 1,000 CFM moves air through a garage or jobsite room. Box fans that plug in move more, but battery fans go anywhere.

Battery only or hybrid?

Hybrid fans (like the RIDGID, CRAFTSMAN CMCE001B and Paxwrenx) run on battery or AC, so you save the battery when an outlet's close.

How long will a battery last?

Small fans run for hours on low. DEWALT lists up to 4.5 hours for the DCE512B and CRAFTSMAN up to 8 hours on high for the CMCE001B, depending on the battery.

Why IP54?

An IP54 rating means it handles dust and splashes, useful in a dusty jobsite or damp garage.

Are the big CFM claims real?

Tool-brand ratings are the most reliable. Treat no-name claims that beat DEWALT and FLEX by a wide margin with suspicion.
