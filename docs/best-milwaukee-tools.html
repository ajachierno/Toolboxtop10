Milwaukee questions

Do Milwaukee M12 and M18 batteries fit each other's tools?

No. M12 and M18 are separate platforms with different battery shapes. M12 tools are smaller and lighter; M18 tools have more power. Most people pick one as their main platform and add the other only for compact jobs.

What's the difference between M18 and M18 FUEL?

M18 FUEL is Milwaukee's premium line, with brushless motors and more power and runtime than standard M18 tools. Both run on the same M18 batteries.

Does PACKOUT work with other brands' tool boxes?

No. PACKOUT pieces lock to each other, not to DEWALT TSTAK or ToughSystem or other brands' modular boxes. If you're already invested in PACKOUT, stay with it; if you're starting fresh, compare it against the other systems on our tool box lists.

Why is there no Milwaukee drill on this page?

Our lists are built from Amazon's best-selling, most-reviewed products, and no Milwaukee drill made our cordless drill top 10 when we last checked. See our cordless drill list for the best from any brand.

Should I buy a bare tool or a kit?

Nearly all the Milwaukee power tools here are bare tools ("tool only"), which is how Milwaukee sells best on Amazon. They make sense if you already own M18 or M12 batteries and a charger. If you don't, add a starter battery kit, or pick a kit from our main lists instead.
