CRAFTSMAN questions

Is CRAFTSMAN V20 the same as 20V MAX?

Yes. V20 is CRAFTSMAN's name for its 20V MAX battery line, and some listings use both names. Like other "20V MAX" tools, it runs at a nominal 18V under load.

Do CRAFTSMAN V20 batteries fit DEWALT tools?

Not officially. CRAFTSMAN and DEWALT are owned by the same company, but their batteries are designed for their own tools. Plan on staying within one platform.

What is CRAFTSMAN V20 RP?

RP marks CRAFTSMAN's brushless V20 tools, its higher-performance range. They use the same V20 batteries as the standard tools.

Should I buy a bare tool or a kit?

If you already own V20 batteries and a charger, buy the bare tool ("tool only"). It's the same tool for less. If this is your first V20 tool, buy a kit so a battery and charger come in the box.

Why isn't CRAFTSMAN the top pick in more categories?

CRAFTSMAN makes our top-10 lists often, but in most categories another brand scores higher on ratings, review volume, or features. Each card says who ranked higher and by how much, so you can decide whether staying with CRAFTSMAN is worth it.
