How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $45; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from blade 22 plus (30%), cut 3 4 (30%), cord retention (20%), adjustable angle (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 15,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Corded or cordless hedge trimmer?

Corded trimmers cost less, never need charging and are usually lighter than a battery model with the same blade. You give up range: you have to stay within an extension cord's reach of an outlet. For hedges along the house or a small yard, corded is the cheaper choice.

What blade length do I need?

16 to 18 inches is enough for shrubs and foundation plantings. For hedges longer than about 10 feet, a 22 to 24 inch blade covers more in each pass. Longer blades weigh more, which you notice when trimming above shoulder height.

What does cut capacity mean?

It's the thickest branch the blade gap will take, usually 5/8 to 3/4 inch on corded trimmers. Anything thicker needs loppers or a pruning saw. The BLACK+DECKER BEHTS300 adds a saw tip rated for branches up to 1.5 inches.

Do I need a pole hedge trimmer?

Only if your hedges are taller than you can reach comfortably from the ground, roughly 6 feet. Pole models weigh 7.5 to 8.9 lb here, against about 3.9 to 7 lb for the handhelds, and the weight sits at the far end of the pole.

What extension cord should I use?

An outdoor-rated cord. For a 3 to 5 amp trimmer, a 16-gauge cord is fine up to 100 feet. Most trimmers here have a cord retainer; loop the cord through it so it doesn't pull out of the trimmer mid-cut, and keep the cord behind you, away from the blade.
