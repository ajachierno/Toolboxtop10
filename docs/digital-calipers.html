Buyer's guide

Steel or plastic calipers?

Stainless steel calipers are accurate to about ±0.001 inch and hold up for years. Plastic calipers read to ±0.01 inch, ten times coarser, and the jaws flex. Plastic is fine for rough measuring; anything with tolerances needs steel.

Accuracy vs resolution?

Resolution is the smallest step the display shows (0.0005 inch on good calipers). Accuracy is how close the reading is to the truth (typically ±0.001 inch). A display can show more digits than the caliper can actually measure.

What does IP54 mean?

IP54 calipers resist dust and splashing water or coolant. Unsealed digital calipers can glitch when dust or moisture gets into the electronics.

Do I need fraction readout?

If you work in fractional inches (woodworking, drill bits), a fraction mode saves converting decimals. Machinists usually prefer decimal inches or millimeters.

How do I get accurate readings?

Clean the jaws, close them and zero before measuring, use light consistent pressure, and measure square to the part. Replace the battery when the display flickers or drifts.
