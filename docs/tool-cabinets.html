How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $120; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from wheels (30%), pegboard (25%), drawer (25%), keyed lock (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 7,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Tool cabinet or tool chest?

A tool chest is a stack of drawers for hand tools and small parts. A tool cabinet is a tall cupboard with shelves for power tools in cases, paint, chemicals and bins. Most garages end up with both.

How much weight can the shelves hold?

Check whether the rating is per shelf or for the whole cabinet. The steel cabinets here are rated 120 to 180 lbs per shelf where the listing says; the Sterilite plastic cabinet is 40 lbs per shelf. Put heavy cases on the bottom shelf either way.

Should I anchor it to the wall?

Yes. A 71-inch cabinet loaded with tools can tip if a door is pulled hard or a child climbs it. Several here include a wall anchor or anti-tip kit; use it, especially on a sloped garage floor.

Are wheels worth it?

Casters let you roll the cabinet out to clean behind it or move it to the work, but they make it less stable. If you add wheels, make sure at least two lock, and anchor the cabinet when it's parked.

What does a 3-point lock mean?

A 3-point lock bolts the door at the top, middle and bottom when you turn the key, so the door can't be pried open at a corner. A single-point lock only latches in the middle.
