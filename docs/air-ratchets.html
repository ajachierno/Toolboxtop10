How we rank

The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $45; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from high torque (30%), variable (25%), light (25%), exhaust 360 (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 1,500 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Air ratchet or impact wrench?

An impact wrench hammers tight bolts loose; an air ratchet spins loose fasteners in and out quickly and fits in tighter spaces. Mechanics use both: break it loose with the impact or a breaker bar, run it out with the ratchet.

Which drive size?

3/8-inch covers most engine work. 1/4-inch palm ratchets fit under dashboards and handle small fasteners. 1/2-inch suits larger bolts if you already own 1/2-inch sockets.

How much air does an air ratchet use?

Usually 3 to 5 CFM at 90 PSI. Ratchets run in short bursts, so a mid-size compressor keeps up easily.

Can I break bolts loose with an air ratchet?

Not tight ones. Most ratchets are rated 25 to 75 ft-lbs. Forcing them past that wears the head out fast. Use a breaker bar or impact wrench first.

Do air ratchets need oil?

Yes. A few drops of air tool oil in the inlet before each use keeps the motor and head working smoothly.
