How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $70; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from a brushless motor (35%), full width (25%), name brand (25%), a complete kit (15%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 5,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

What is a cordless hand planer for?

Taking a little wood off a narrow edge, fast: trimming a sticking door, scribing a cabinet to a wavy wall, easing a deck board, or straightening a stud. It isn't a thickness planer. It won't make a wide board flat or bring it to an exact thickness.

3-1/4 in or a compact 2 in planer?

Most cordless planers cut 3-1/4 in wide, which covers a 2x4 edge and any door edge in one pass. The 12V Milwaukee (2 in) and Bosch (2.2 in) are lighter and easier to hold one-handed, but they need two passes on anything wider than a door.

Bare tool or kit?

Nearly every cordless planer is sold bare. If you already own DEWALT, Makita, Milwaukee, Bosch or CRAFTSMAN batteries, buy the bare tool for that platform. If you own none, a kit like the WEN 20653 is cheaper than buying a name-brand tool plus a battery and charger.

Are third-party planers that fit DEWALT or Makita batteries any good?

For occasional use, the better ones (MtiolHig, TengXcel) rate 4.3 to 4.4 stars. They have fewer reviews, no clear warranty and no parts network, so a daily user should buy the name brand.

How deep should each pass be?

Most models top out at 5/64 in (2 mm); the Bosch 18V goes to 3/32 in and the WEN to 1/8 in. Deep passes drain the battery and tear out more. Take several light passes on hardwood and finish with the shallowest setting.

Does brushless matter on a planer?

A brushless motor gets more runtime from the same battery and needs no brush changes. Every planer here is brushless except the Makita XPK01Z, which makes up for it with carbide blades and a long record.
