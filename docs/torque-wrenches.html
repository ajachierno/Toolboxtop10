How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $30; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from accuracy 3 (35%), dual direction (25%), certificate (20%), a carrying case (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 20,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Which drive size do I need?

1/2-inch for lug nuts, suspension and anything over about 80 ft-lb. 3/8-inch for spark plugs and most engine bolts. 1/4-inch, rated in inch-pounds, for bikes and small fasteners. A torque wrench is least accurate at the bottom fifth of its range, so pick one where your spec sits in the middle.

Click, micrometer or digital?

Click wrenches (most of this list) break over with a click when you hit the setting. Micrometer wrenches are click wrenches with a finer, locking scale. Digital wrenches read out the torque on a screen and warn you as you approach it; they're more precise but need batteries.

What does ±4% accuracy mean?

At a 100 ft-lb setting, the wrench may click anywhere from 96 to 104 ft-lb. That's within the ASME standard for click wrenches and fine for wheels and most car work. ±3% or ±2% matters more on engine internals.

Do I need a wrench that measures both directions?

Only for left-hand threads, such as some bike pedals, axle nuts and fan clutches. Most wrenches ratchet both ways but only measure clockwise.

How do I keep a torque wrench accurate?

Wind it back to the lowest setting after use, never use it to break bolts loose, and don't drop it. Have it checked or recalibrated every year or so if you use it often.
