The list is ordered by that score. The features score is built from green (25%), plane 360 (25%), rechargeable (20%), pulse (15%), tripod (15%), capped at 100. Buyer's guide

Cross-line or 360-degree?

A cross-line laser projects one horizontal and one vertical line on the wall in front of it, which covers pictures, shelves and a run of cabinets. A 3x360 laser wraps level and plumb lines around the whole room, which helps with ceilings, wall-to-wall tile and framing.

Green or red beam?

Green beams look about 2 to 4 times brighter to the eye, so they're easier to see in lit rooms and at distance. Red lasers cost less and run longer on batteries.

What does self-leveling mean?

A pendulum inside swings the laser level by itself within about 3-4 degrees. If you set it on an uneven surface beyond that, it blinks to warn you. Lock the pendulum before moving it.

What is pulse mode?

Outdoors or in bright sun you often can't see the beam. Pulse mode makes the laser flash at a rate a laser detector (sold separately) can pick up, extending the range to 150 feet or more.

How accurate does a laser level need to be?

About 1/8 inch at 30 feet is the common standard and fine for cabinets and tile. Many budget lasers don't publish an accuracy figure at all; check one against a known level line before relying on it.

AikTryee Laser Level Line Tool, Cross Line Laser Leveler Beam Tool with 8ft Tape

A $10 laser pointer with bubble vials, sold as a laser level. For $25 more, the Lusweimi actually levels itself and lists its accuracy.

Why we'd skip it

  • It isn't self-leveling. You line up a bubble vial by eye while it's stuck to the wall, so the line is only as level as your read of the bubble, which is exactly the error a laser level is supposed to remove.
  • The listing gives no accuracy figure at all, even though it's sold for cabinets and tile, the jobs where 1/8 inch matters.
  • It projects the line from where it's mounted on the wall, so it can't throw a level line across a room or onto an opposite wall like the self-leveling lasers above.
