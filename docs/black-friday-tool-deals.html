Black Friday tool questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving. Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026. Amazon typically starts early Black Friday pricing on tools in the week before.

Are Black Friday tool deals actually good?

Some are, many aren't. Cordless kits from the big brands (DEWALT, Milwaukee, RYOBI, Makita) often get real cuts or free-battery bundles. But some "deals" are special-edition kits that never sold at the crossed-out price. Compare against the regular price we list here before you buy.

Should I buy a bare tool or a kit on Black Friday?

If you don't already own batteries for that brand, buy a kit. The batteries and charger are the expensive part, and Black Friday kit bundles are where the biggest savings usually are. If you're already on a battery platform, bare tools are the cheaper way to add to it.

Is it better to buy tools on Black Friday or Cyber Monday?

For tools, Black Friday week usually has the widest selection of discounts. Cyber Monday often repeats the same prices and sometimes adds a few new ones. If your pick hits a price you're happy with before either day, buy it — popular kits can sell out.
