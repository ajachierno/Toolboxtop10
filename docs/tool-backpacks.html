How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 50% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 15% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $45; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from a hard, waterproof base (35%), water resistance (25%), laptop sleeve (20%), removable panel (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 6,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Tool backpack or tool bag?

A backpack spreads the weight across both shoulders and frees your hands, which matters on ladders, stairs and long walks from the truck. A tote or shoulder bag is faster to dig through and easier to set down open. If you carry more than about 15 lb of tools or climb a lot, get the backpack.

Does a molded base matter?

Yes, if you set the bag on wet ground, concrete or a roof. A molded plastic base keeps water out, stops the bottom wearing through and lets the bag stand on its own. Nine of the ten picks here have one; the CLC 1132 listing doesn't mention one.

How many pockets do I need?

Count your hand tools and add a few. Electricians and HVAC techs with lots of drivers, pliers and meters use 40 or more. If you carry a drill, a few bits and a tape measure, 12 to 25 is plenty, and fewer pockets usually means a lighter bag.

What does 1680D mean?

Denier (D) is the thickness of the yarn in the fabric. 1680D ballistic polyester or nylon is the heavy jobsite standard. 600D, used on the AWP Extreme's main body, is lighter and cheaper but wears through sooner under daily use.

How heavy is an empty tool backpack?

Most of the bags here that list a weight run 4.3 to 9 lb empty. The Klein 55485 is 13.2 lb because of its removable caddy and cable-reinforced handle. Check the empty weight if you plan to fill it with power tools, since a full pack easily passes 30 lb.
