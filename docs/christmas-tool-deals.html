Christmas tool gift questions

When should I order tools for Christmas 2026?

As early in December as you can. Christmas falls on a Friday this year, so the days right before it are busy shipping weekdays. Go by the delivery date Amazon shows on the listing at checkout instead of assuming fast shipping holds the week of Christmas.

Are tool prices better on Black Friday or closer to Christmas?

Black Friday week (November 27 through Cyber Monday, November 30) usually has the widest run of tool discounts. Some of those prices hold into December and some don't. Either way, compare against the regular price we list: if today's price is below it, it's a fair deal whenever you buy.

Should I give a bare tool or a kit?

Give a kit unless you know which battery brand the person already uses. A bare tool doesn't run without a battery and charger from the same platform. If they're already on DEWALT, Milwaukee, RYOBI or Makita, a bare tool from that brand is the cheaper gift.

What if I pick the wrong tool?

Amazon usually extends its return window for items bought in November and December into late January, but the dates change from year to year and some third-party sellers set their own rules. Check the return policy on the listing before you buy.
