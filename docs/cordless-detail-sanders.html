How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $50; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from a complete kit (30%), a brushless motor (25%), a vacuum port (25%), variable speed (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 2,500 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

What's the difference between a detail sander and a random orbital sander?

A detail (mouse) sander has a pointed, iron-shaped pad that reaches into corners and along trim. A random orbital sander has a round 5-inch pad that removes material faster on flat surfaces but can't get into a 90-degree corner. Many people own both.

Should I buy a kit or a bare tool?

If you already own batteries for BLACK+DECKER, CRAFTSMAN, WORX or DeWalt, buy the bare tool and use what you have. If you don't, a kit is cheaper than buying the tool, battery and charger separately. On this page, the BLACK+DECKER kit is only $5 more than its bare tool.

Is there a DeWalt, Milwaukee or Makita cordless mouse sander?

DeWalt doesn't sell a mouse sander. Milwaukee's M12 FUEL orbital detail sander exists, but Amazon had no seller for it when we checked. For DeWalt 20V batteries, third-party tools like the Mellif and TRONYAN on this page fill the gap. They aren't made or backed by DeWalt.

Does battery size matter on a detail sander?

It sets runtime. A 1.3Ah or 1.5Ah pack is fine for a few doors or a chair; a 2.0Ah pack lasts longer between charges. If you plan to sand a whole room of trim, get a spare battery or a kit with a 2.0Ah pack.

Do I need a vacuum port?

For small jobs the dust bag is enough. For long sessions indoors, a shop-vac connection pulls far more dust off the work. On this page only the CRAFTSMAN CMCW221B lists a 1-1/4-inch vacuum connection.
