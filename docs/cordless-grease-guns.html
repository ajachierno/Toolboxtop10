How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $110; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from a complete kit (30%), long hose (25%), speed control (25%), an LED work light (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 7,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

How much pressure do I need?

Most grease fittings take far less than 10,000 PSI. The high rating matters for clogged or seized fittings, where a manual gun can't push through. Every gun here except the M12 is rated 10,000 PSI or more.

What does flow rate change?

Flow (oz per minute) is how fast grease comes out. Higher flow fills big bearings and equipment faster. DEWALT lists 5 oz/min; RYOBI and CRAFTSMAN claim 7.5 to 9 oz/min.

Bare tool or kit?

If you own batteries from the same brand, buy the bare tool. If you don't, a kit from DEWALT or Milwaukee costs more but leaves you on a platform you can grow into. The no-name kits are cheapest but their batteries fit nothing else.

Cartridge or bulk grease?

All of these take standard 14 oz cartridges. Many can also be filled from a bulk tub, which is cheaper if you grease a lot of equipment.

How long a hose do I need?

Forty inches or more lets you reach fittings under a mower deck or up on a loader arm without moving the gun. The RYOBI's 30-inch hose is the shortest here.
