How we rank

The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $1300; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from standing height (30%), portable (20%), runs 110v (20%), load tested (15%), long warranty (15%), capped at 100. Buyer's guide

Portable lift, mid-rise, 2-post, or 4-post?

Portable low-rise lifts (about 27 to 28 in.) need no installation and store on a wall, but you work lying on a creeper. Mid-rise scissor lifts (about 39 in.) let you sit on a stool. 2-post lifts raise the car to standing height and leave the wheels hanging for brake and suspension work, but they must be anchored in solid concrete. 4-post lifts are drive-on, double as parking storage, and don't need anchoring.

How high does my ceiling need to be?

For a portable or mid-rise lift, a normal 8 ft garage is fine. For standing-height work, add the lift's max height, the car's roof height, and a few inches of clearance. The APlusLift 4-post is 94 in. tall before it lifts anything, and overhead 2-posts need room for the top beam. Most people need 11 to 12 ft for a 2-post or 4-post.

Can my garage floor take a 2-post lift?

2-post lifts bolt into the slab, and most makers call for at least 4 in. of 3,000 psi concrete with no cracks or expansion joints under the posts. If you don't know your slab, drill a test hole first. Portable and 4-post lifts sit on the floor and don't need anchoring.

110V or 220V?

The portable lifts and the APlusLift and KATOOL 4-posts here run on 110V. Many 2-post lifts, including the XK, need a 220V circuit and a 30 A breaker, which may mean paying an electrician.

How do I get it off the truck?

Portable lifts ship by parcel or LTL to the curb. Full 2-post and 4-post lifts often weigh 1,000 to 1,800 lb crated, and several sellers here require you to unload with a forklift or tractor, or pick up at a freight terminal. APlusLift includes liftgate service on some models; check before you order.
