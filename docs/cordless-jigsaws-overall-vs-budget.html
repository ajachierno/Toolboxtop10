The short answer

The Seesii 2x2.0Ah Kit costs $4.39 more (7% more) than the BLACK+DECKER BDCJS20C — $64.59 vs $60.20. For the extra money you get: an LED work light. Meanwhile the BLACK+DECKER BDCJS20C has a dust blower, which the Seesii 2x2.0Ah Kit doesn't.

Buyers rate them 4.7 vs 4.6 stars, and the Seesii 2x2.0Ah Kit has fewer reviews behind its rating (282 vs 3,804). On our 100-point scale that works out to 87 vs 86.

Bottom line: only 1 point separate them, so the BLACK+DECKER BDCJS20C is the smarter buy unless you specifically need an LED work light.
