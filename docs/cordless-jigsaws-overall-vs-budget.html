DEWALT DCS334B vs WORX WX550L

Our Best Overall vs our Best Budget cordless jigsaws — is the upgrade worth it?

The short answer

The DEWALT DCS334B costs $59.69 more (86% more) than the WORX WX550L — $129 vs $69.31. For the extra money you get: a brushless motor. Meanwhile the WORX WX550L has a complete kit (everything you need in the box), which the DEWALT DCS334B doesn't.

Buyers rate them 4.8 vs 4.6 stars, and the DEWALT DCS334B has more reviews behind its rating (9,642 vs 4,011). On our 100-point scale that works out to 92 vs 87.

Bottom line: if you'll use it often, the DEWALT DCS334B is worth the $59.69. For occasional jobs around the house, the WORX WX550L does the work for less.
