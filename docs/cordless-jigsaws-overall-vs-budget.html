DEWALT DCS334B vs BLACK+DECKER BDCJS20C

Our Best Overall vs our Best Budget cordless jigsaws — is the upgrade worth it?

The short answer

The DEWALT DCS334B costs $43 more (50% more) than the BLACK+DECKER BDCJS20C — $129 vs $86. For the extra money you get: a brushless motor and an LED work light. Meanwhile the BLACK+DECKER BDCJS20C has a complete kit (everything you need in the box), which the DEWALT DCS334B doesn't.

Buyers rate them 4.8 vs 4.6 stars, and the DEWALT DCS334B has more reviews behind its rating (9,623 vs 3,801). On our 100-point scale that works out to 92 vs 83.

Bottom line: if you'll use it often, the DEWALT DCS334B is worth the $43. For occasional jobs around the house, the BLACK+DECKER BDCJS20C does the work for less.
