Makita questions

Do Makita 18V LXT, 40V XGT and 12V CXT batteries interchange?

No. They're three separate platforms, and each battery fits only its own line of tools. 18V LXT is Makita's largest platform, and it's the one every Makita cordless tool on this page uses.

What does the "Z" at the end of a Makita model number mean?

It means tool only: no battery or charger in the box. For example, XAG04Z is the bare angle grinder. That's the cheaper way to buy if you already own LXT batteries.

Why is there no Makita drill or impact driver on this page?

Our lists are built from Amazon's best-selling, most-reviewed products, and no Makita drill or impact driver made those top 10s when we last checked. See our cordless drill and impact driver lists for the best from any brand.

Why are so many of these Makita tools corded?

Because that's where Makita scores best in our data. Its corded angle grinder, random orbit sander, and jigsaw rank near the top of their lists, and corded tools don't tie you to any battery platform.
