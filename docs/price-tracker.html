How this is measured

A check is the price Amazon showed for a listing on the day one of our pages was refreshed. Each page is re-priced about weekly, so most tools have one check per week. The 30-day change compares the latest check with the last check taken at least 30 days before the site's newest capture; a tool counts only if its own latest check is within 10 days of that capture, and tools without a check that old are left out rather than guessed. Moves under 3% or under $2 are treated as noise. A monthly report compares each tool's last check before the month with its last check inside it. We only track tools currently ranked on the site; when one is removed, its history stops.
