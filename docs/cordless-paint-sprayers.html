How we rank

The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $50; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from a complete kit (30%), adjustable flow (25%), no thinning (25%), a brushless motor (20%), capped at 100. Buyer's guide

HVLP or airless: which cordless sprayer do I need?

Handheld HVLP guns (most of this list, $40 to $100) blow low-pressure air to atomize paint. They're good for furniture, cabinets, doors and fences, but most need thinned paint and refill every quart or so. Cordless airless sprayers like the Graco Ultra Max and Titan ControlMax pump paint at high pressure, cover faster and handle thicker coatings, but cost $600 and up.

Do I have to thin the paint?

With almost every handheld HVLP here, yes. Use the viscosity cup in the box and thin until the paint runs through in the time the manual gives. The listing's DIN/s rating tells you how thick a coating it can take: 80 DIN/s handles more than 40 DIN/s. Of the sprayers on this page, only the Graco Ultra Max is sold as spraying without thinning.

Will a 'compatible with DEWALT' sprayer void anything or damage my batteries?

These are third-party tools built to fit DEWALT 20V or RYOBI 18V packs; DEWALT and RYOBI don't make or warrant them. Most include a low-voltage cutoff so they won't over-drain the pack. Check the listing's battery list: both Tilswall listings say they don't fit DEWALT POWERSTACK batteries.

Tool-only or a kit with batteries?

If you already own DEWALT 20V or RYOBI 18V batteries, a tool-only sprayer is the cheapest option. If you don't, a kit like the NANWEI ($59.99 with two batteries and a charger) costs less than buying a tool-only sprayer plus a name-brand battery and charger. Kit batteries are usually proprietary and won't run other tools.

How long does a battery last when spraying?

Runtime depends on battery size and the motor. PEMMKORE lists up to 25 minutes per 4.0Ah battery, and Titan rates each 2Ah pack at about 350 sq ft per charge on the ControlMax. Bigger packs run longer, so a 4.0Ah or 5.0Ah battery is worth it for fences and decks.

How do I keep a cordless sprayer from clogging?

Strain the paint, thin it to the right viscosity, and clean the sprayer right after you finish. Tilswall says to clean within 15 minutes. Spray water (or the right solvent) through it, take the nozzle apart, and clear the air hole with the needle that comes in the box.
