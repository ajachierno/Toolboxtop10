The 10 Best Corded Detail Sanders

Mouse and detail sanders with pointed pads for corners, trim, spindles and furniture, ranked from live Amazon data.

Ranked by ToolboxTop10 from Amazon data captured Oct 10, 2026. How we rank

A detail sander (often called a mouse sander) has an iron-shaped pad that reaches into corners, along trim and between spindles where a round orbital pad can't. We ranked 10 corded best-sellers on rating, review volume and features (extra detail attachments, a vacuum port and hook-and-loop paper). Most run 12,000 to 16,000 orbits per minute, so the real differences are attachments, dust collection and the sheets in the box. They run from about $21 for WEN's 6301 to $59 for CRAFTSMAN's CMEW210.

How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $30; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from attachments (35%), hook loop (35%), a vacuum port (30%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 20,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

Detail sander or random orbital sander?

A random orbital sander is faster and leaves a better finish on flat panels. A detail sander's pointed pad gets into corners, along trim and between spindles. Many people own both.

Do I need the extra attachments?

Only for furniture and trim. A finger attachment gets between chair slats and spindles; a contour pad follows curves. For corners and edges, the standard pad is enough.

Does orbits per minute matter?

A little. Faster pads remove material quicker, but every sander here runs 12,000 to 16,000 opm. Dust collection and paper quality make more difference.

Can I use regular sandpaper?

Detail sanders use pre-cut hook-and-loop sheets shaped like the pad. Buy sheets that match the pad size and hole pattern so dust collection works.

Will a detail sander strip paint?

It will for small areas like window frames and trim, with 60 or 80 grit. For large painted surfaces a random orbital or belt sander is much faster. Test old paint for lead before sanding it.
