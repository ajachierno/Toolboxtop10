The short answer

The DEWALT DCD771C2 costs $72.01 more (153% more) than the AVID POWER ACD305 — $119 vs $46.99. For the extra money you get: a 2-speed gearbox and two batteries in the box. Meanwhile the AVID POWER ACD305 has an LED work light, which the DEWALT DCD771C2 doesn't.

Buyers rate them 4.8 vs 4.6 stars, and the DEWALT DCD771C2 has more reviews behind its rating (49,788 vs 23,553). On our 100-point scale that works out to 88 vs 79.

Bottom line: if you'll use it often, the DEWALT DCD771C2 is worth the $72.01. For occasional jobs around the house, the AVID POWER ACD305 does the work for less.
