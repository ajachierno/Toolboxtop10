How we rank

Every tool gets one score from 0 to 100, weighted 45% on its star rating, 35% on how many people have reviewed it (more reviews = more confidence the rating is real), and 20% on the features that matter for the job. The list is ordered by that score. Best Overall is our pick for the best all-around, ready-to-use tool; Best Budget the best value at or under $90; and Money No Object the one to buy if price is no object.

The features score is built from wide swath (30%), auto feed (25%), battery included (25%), edger (20%), capped at 100. The review-count score is on a log scale, so each tenfold jump in reviews adds the same amount, and 30,000 or more reviews gets full marks. Open Why this score on any tool to see its numbers.

Buyer's guide

How wide a swath do I need?

10 to 12 inches suits small yards and edges. 13 to 16 inches finishes bigger properties faster and handles thicker growth.

What does line thickness change?

Thicker line (.080 to .095 in) cuts weeds and tough grass without snapping. Thin line (.065 in and below) is fine for grass edges and small yards.

Auto feed or bump feed?

Auto feed advances line without tapping the head on the ground. EGO's POWERLOAD winds new line at the push of a button. Both save time over refilling spools by hand.

Do I need an edger mode?

If you want clean lines along sidewalks and driveways, yes. 2-in-1 trimmers rotate the head and add a wheel for edging.

20V, 40V or 56V?

20V is light and fine for small yards. 40V and EGO's 56V run longer and cut thicker growth. Pick the platform you'll use for other yard tools.
