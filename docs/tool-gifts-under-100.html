Gift-buying questions

What is the best tool gift under $100?

For most people, a cordless drill kit with a battery and charger. It's the tool that gets used most around the house. If they already own one, a cordless impact driver on the same battery platform, or a big mechanics socket set, is the next step up.

Should I buy a cordless tool from the brand they already own?

Yes, if you know it. Batteries only fit tools from the same brand (and usually the same voltage line). A tool from their brand means they can share batteries, and the kits here come with their own battery, so any pick works if you're not sure.

Where are the gifts under $50?
