Gift-buying questions

What is the best tool gift under $100?

For most people, a cordless drill kit with a battery and charger. It's the tool that gets used most around the house. If they already own one, a cordless impact driver on the same battery platform, or a big mechanics socket set, is the next step up.

Should I buy a cordless tool from the brand they already own?

Yes, if you know it. Batteries only fit tools from the same brand (and usually the same voltage line). A tool from their brand means they can share batteries, and the kits here come with their own battery, so any pick works if you're not sure.

What's a good tool gift under $100 for a car lover?

A lithium jump starter, an OBD2 scanner with live data, a cordless tire inflator, a dual-action car polisher or a 3-ton floor jack. The garage section of this page has a pick for each.

What about a woodworker?

Look at the shop tools: a benchtop drill press, an oscillating spindle sander, a Dremel rotary tool kit, a compact router or a set of parallel clamps. Clamps are the one tool woodworkers never have enough of.

Some picks need an air compressor. Are they still good gifts?

Only if the person already has a compressor. Those cards are marked "Needs an air compressor". If you're not sure, choose a corded or battery tool instead.
